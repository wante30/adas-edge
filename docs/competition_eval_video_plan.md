# Competition road-video evaluation plan

Prepare 6–10 short clips, preferably 20–60 seconds at 720p/1080p and 25–30 fps:

- intersection daylight
- traffic light
- pedestrian crossing
- dense traffic
- normal dashcam
- night road
- road obstacle
- moving camera

Priority order: official/competition material first; team-recorded or existing footage second; legally usable public road video third. Do not download a large dataset for this acceptance phase. The evaluator is ready, but no result is valid until actual clips are supplied and recorded in `data/competition_eval/manifest.csv`.
